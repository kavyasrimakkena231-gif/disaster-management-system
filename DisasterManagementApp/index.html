About the Flood

Heavy rainfall can cause rivers and drainage systems to overflow, resulting in flooding in low-lying areas of Kerala.

📍 Affected Location

The incident information identifies Kerala, India as the affected region. Vulnerable low-lying and riverside areas should be monitored closely during heavy rainfall.

Safety Information

  • Move to higher ground if water levels rise.

  • Keep emergency supplies and medicines ready.
